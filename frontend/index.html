Our story

A small kitchen with long memories

Foodies began in 2012 as a twelve-seat room off Carter Road, run by a family that cooked the way they ate at home — slowly, generously, without shortcuts. The room is bigger now, the menu a little longer, but the kitchen still works the same way.

Gravies are still simmered for hours. Breads are still rolled by hand and slapped into a clay oven. Masalas are still ground in-house every morning, because the smell of freshly roasted spice is the first thing we want you to notice.
